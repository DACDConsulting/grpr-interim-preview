Ruth Ann Daniels

Partner, Gray Reed · Labor & Employment

Attorney at Gray Reed. Not a GRPR consultant.

Ruth Ann Daniels leads Gray Reed’s Labor and Employment group. She advises employers on workplace disputes, investigations, and the day-to-day issues that can become a public matter.

Her work includes discrimination, harassment, retaliation, and non-compete disputes, in court and in arbitration. On a crisis, she is the employment counterpart to the communications team.

J.D., cum laude, Baylor Law School. B.A., magna cum laude, Baylor University. Admitted in Texas.
