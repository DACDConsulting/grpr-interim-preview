Philip Jordan

Partner, Gray Reed · Energy

Attorney at Gray Reed. Not a GRPR consultant.

Philip Jordan leads Gray Reed’s Energy group. He is board certified in oil, gas, and mineral law and advises on acquisitions, midstream agreements, and project finance.

Before joining the firm he was general counsel of an independent exploration company. On data center and energy matters he covers the transactions around power and fuel supply.

J.D., summa cum laude, South Texas College of Law Houston. B.A., Stephen F. Austin State University. Admitted in Texas.
