Why clients choose GRPR

  • 01

    One plan, two disciplines

    Government affairs and public relations sit on the same team and work from the same strategy. Every meeting at the Capitol and every message in the market pulls in the same direction.

  • 02

    Session-tested at the Texas Capitol

    Our government affairs practice is led by a former Texas Senate chief of staff and was recognized in Capitol Inside’s 2025 Texas Lobby Power Rankings. We know the process, the people and the calendar.
