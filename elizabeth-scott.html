Elizabeth Scott

Strategic Communications Coordinator

Elizabeth Scott serves as Strategic Communications Coordinator at GRPR, supporting work that helps clients communicate with clarity, strengthen stakeholder relationships, and protect and advance their reputations.

She contributes across research, message development, client deliverables, media and social content, and business development, with a thoughtful, organized, and audience-focused approach.

Before joining GRPR, Elizabeth worked across agency marketing, congressional communications, and higher education. At 101 Media she helped develop integrated public relations and marketing campaigns, turned strategy discussions into content direction, and supported new business outreach.

She previously served in the Washington, D.C., office of U.S. Congressman August Pfluger, where she managed constituent communications, conducted policy research, prepared written materials, and led public-facing Capitol tours. She also supported strategy and communications at the SMU Cox Career Management Center.

Elizabeth earned a Bachelor of Business Administration in Marketing from the SMU Cox School of Business and a Bachelor of Arts in Public Relations and Strategic Communications from the SMU Meadows School of the Arts. At SMU she founded a private student advisory council to the Dean of the Cox School of Business, represented more than 300 students in the SMU Student Senate, and led employer outreach for the Cox Marketing Academy.

A lifelong Houstonian, she spends time with her family in South Texas.
