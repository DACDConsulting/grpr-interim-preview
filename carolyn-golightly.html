Carolyn Golightly

Public Relations Project Specialist

Carolyn Golightly serves as Public Relations Project Specialist at GRPR, a strategic partner to the leadership team. She oversees high-priority projects, manages executive workflows and keeps teams aligned so client work is delivered with care.

Her experience spans national organizations and local operations. She has led strategic planning sessions, supported leadership transitions and facilitated complex decisions for senior executives. Certifications in customer success and project management inform how she runs the work.

She researches, writes and develops messages for op-eds, keynote speeches, national campaigns and high-profile events. From fundraising retreats to groundbreaking ceremonies, she keeps the detail in line with the purpose.

Known for calm leadership under pressure, she asks useful questions and works toward a shared outcome. The daughter of an Army soldier, she grew up with a wide view of the world. She holds a bachelor's degree in International Studies from Texas A&M University and a Master of Divinity in Biblical Counseling from Southeastern Baptist Theological Seminary.

Outside of GRPR she contributes to her community through speaking, teaching and music.
