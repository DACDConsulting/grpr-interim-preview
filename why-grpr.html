Why GRPR

The Capitol and the headline. One team.

Government Affairs + Public Relations

Most organizations hire a lobbyist, a PR agency and outside counsel, then hope they talk to each other. GRPR puts government affairs and strategic communications on one plan, with Gray Reed’s attorneys ready when the stakes turn legal.

The problem

Policy and perception
are the same fight.

A bill in committee becomes a story in the paper. A local news segment becomes a question at a hearing. A workplace complaint becomes a regulatory inquiry. Texas issues cross the line between policy and public opinion every day.

Most organizations still manage them in separate lanes. The lobbyist doesn’t see the press plan. The PR agency doesn’t know what’s moving in committee. Legal counsel gets the call last. By the time everyone is in the same room, someone else has set the narrative.

We built GRPR so you never have to coordinate your advisors in the middle of a fight.

What sets us apart

Six reasons clients
choose GRPR.

Policy outcomes and public perception rarely come apart. We plan them together, with the relationships and the legal backing to follow through.

01

One plan, two disciplines

Government affairs and public relations sit on the same team and work from the same strategy. Every meeting at the Capitol and every message in the market pulls in the same direction.

02

Session-tested at the Texas Capitol

Our government affairs practice is led by a former Texas Senate chief of staff and was recognized in Capitol Inside’s 2025 Texas Lobby Power Rankings. We know the process and the calendar.

03

A separate firm

GRPR is a division of Gray Reed Advisory Services, a wholly owned subsidiary of Gray Reed.

04

Built for the hardest moments

Through Crisis Command, our GRPR professionals and Gray Reed’s attorneys handle readiness, response and recovery as one team, starting in the first 15 minutes.

05

Ahead of the issue

We track the bills, hearings and rule-makings that matter to you, during session and the interim, and publish on emerging issues before they peak. Data center water use is one recent example.

06

Senior people do the work

The people you meet at the start are the people who run your engagement. You get direct access to the leaders of our practices, not a hand-off to a junior account team.

When to call

When clients call us

Our work usually starts with a moment where policy, public opinion and business risk collide.

The best time to call is before the issue has a name. The second-best time is now.

  1. A bill or amendment threatens your business model
  2. An agency opens a rule-making or permit review that affects you
  3. A project faces community or local opposition
  4. A workplace, safety or cyber incident is about to go public
  5. An executive faces a hostile interview or a legislative hearing
  6. You’re entering the Texas market and need relationships fast
