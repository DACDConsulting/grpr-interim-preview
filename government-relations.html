Services

Government Relations

We develop and execute advocacy plans aligned to your policy goals — and give you the intelligence to act before the landscape shifts.

Well-connected, session-tested and focused on outcomes. Our team identifies, cultivates and leverages relationships with key decision makers across all branches of Texas government, and steers clients through dynamic regulatory and legislative processes.

What we do

  • 01

    Strategy

    With a comprehensive understanding of the regulatory and parliamentary process, we develop a plan tailored to each client’s regulatory or policy goals.

  • 02

    Advocacy

    Meaningful engagement with policymakers and staff is key to legislative success. We approach each meeting with focus — articulating why legislation matters to your business, your industry and the state of Texas.

  • 03

    Legislative Monitoring

    Thousands of bills and amendments are filed every session. We use technology and disciplined tracking to monitor legislation, hearings and rule-making during session and the interim.

  • 04

    Regulatory & Agency Engagement

    We work with state agencies through rulemaking and implementation to make sure policy works for your business once it leaves the Capitol.

  • 05

    Coalitions & Stakeholders

    Policy is rarely won alone. We identify allies, build and strengthen coalitions, and align industry, community and business voices behind a shared message.
