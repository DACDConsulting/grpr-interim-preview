# GRPR Interim Website: Creative Brief
grprpublicaffairs.com · Prepared September 28, 2026 · Owner: Matthew Childress

## 1. Project summary

Refresh grprpublicaffairs.com as an **interim site** that matches the new Gray Reed look: crisp, white and precise, with the Gray Reed red logo as the main accent. The site should read as the public affairs arm of a large, established Texas law firm. It bridges the gap until the full site rebuild (the v4 content architecture).

**The job:** make GRPR presentable, consistent with Gray Reed, and focused. It offers two core disciplines, government affairs and public relations, plus two integrated solutions delivered with Gray Reed attorneys: Crisis Command and GRIDS.

## 2. Background

- GRPR is the government relations and strategic communications division of Gray Reed Advisory Services. It's led by Marc Rylander (PR) and Adam Leggett (GA).
- GRPR drives the largest share of Advisory revenue. It is the engine of the Advisory business.
- The 2027 strategy calls for "depth, not breadth," consistent branding, and a web presence that wins visibility with clients and in AI answer engines.
- The current site uses a blue and gray palette that doesn't match Gray Reed. It has three thin pages and no thought leadership, proof points or integrated offerings.

## 3. Objectives

1. **Align with the brand.** Visually part of the Gray Reed family, with the same logo, red, type direction and restraint.
2. **Clarify the offering.** Two disciplines, one strategy, plus Crisis Command and GRIDS.
3. **Support business development.** A clear path to contact from every page, with team members easy to find.
4. **Establish thought leadership.** An Insights hub with articles, alerts and a Marc Rylander video series that can grow.
5. **Be easy to maintain.** Plain HTML that marketing can update and deploy by hand.

**Success measures** (from the 2027 marketing plan): web traffic, inbound inquiries, non-branded AI citation share, and firm-sourced referrals tracked in HubSpot.

## 4. Audiences

|---|---|

## 5. Positioning and messages

**Positioning:** GRPR helps organizations navigate evolving political and public landscapes, from the Texas Capitol to the front page, backed by Gray Reed.

**Message pillars**

- **Government + Communications.** Policy outcomes and public perception are planned together.
- **Access + Judgment.** A top-ranked Texas lobby team (Capitol Inside 2025) and seasoned communicators.
- **Law firm behind every engagement.** Coordinated with Gray Reed attorneys when legal risk is in play.
- **One coordinated approach, not five vendors.** Crisis Command and GRIDS.

**Headlines in use**

- "Influence the policy. Own the story." (overview slide)
- "Be heard at the Capitol." (Government Affairs slide)
- "Own the story before it owns you." (Public Relations slide)

## 6. Tone of voice

Confident, direct and senior. Short sentences. Specific language over adjectives. No hype, no jargon. It should sound like a trusted advisor in the room, not an agency pitch.

## 7. Visual direction

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Overall feel | Crisp white, generous but disciplined spacing, fine gray dividers, large light headlines |
| Color | White base. **Gray Reed logo red #A91D37** as the only accent. Charcoal #2B2E34 for the closing section and dark bands. Neutral grays for text. |
| Typography | **Gibson** (the Gray Reed web font). Light weight for headlines, semibold for labels. Figtree is the fallback until licensing. |
| Logo | Existing GRPR and Gray Reed logos, unchanged |
| Signature motif | The three red bars from the logo, used as small markers above section labels. A thin red accent on imagery. |
| Imagery | **Pencil-sketch treatment** of Texas photography, on the home page only. Austin skyline for the overview, Texas Capitol for Government Affairs, Dallas bridge for Public Relations. Interior pages are text-led with no hero imagery. |
| Motion | Home hero slider with a crossfade every 7 seconds and progress tabs. Subtle fade-in on scroll. Respects visitors' reduced-motion settings. |
| Mobile | On phones the image sits first, then the tabs, then the copy. Swipe to change slides. Full-screen menu. Tested from 320 to 768 px. |

## 9. Deliverables

- A 12-page responsive HTML/CSS/JS site, ready to upload.
- Editable source (`build.py`), shared stylesheet and README.
- Content gaps and request list (separate PDF).

## 10. Requirements and constraints

- **Legal:** the GRPR disclaimer (not lawyers, privilege) must appear on Crisis Command and GRIDS. Gray Reed compliance must sign off on any description of legal services or matters.
- **Proof points:** nothing mocked goes live. Placeholder stats, stories and testimonials are either replaced or removed before launch.
- **Assets:** logos, photos and fonts must be hosted locally before the old WordPress site is retired.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard-operable slider and menus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, 44 px tap targets.
- **SEO/AEO:** descriptive titles and descriptions, transcripts for videos, and 301 redirects from the old URLs.
