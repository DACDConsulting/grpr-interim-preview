The problem

Policy and perception
are the same fight.

A bill in committee becomes a story in the paper. A local news segment becomes a question at a hearing. A workplace complaint becomes a regulatory inquiry. Texas issues cross the line between policy and public opinion every day.

Most organizations still manage them in separate lanes. The lobbyist doesn’t see the press plan. The PR agency doesn’t know what’s moving in committee. Legal counsel gets the call last. By the time everyone is in the same room, someone else has set the narrative.

We built GRPR so you never have to coordinate your advisors in the middle of a fight.
