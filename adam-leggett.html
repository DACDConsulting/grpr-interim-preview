Adam Leggett

Managing Director of Government Affairs

Adam Leggett is a respected government professional with two decades of public service and an extensive knowledge of the legislative process at the state and federal levels.

Prior to joining GRPR, Adam served in senior roles in the Texas legislature, the Office of the Governor, and as a trusted aide in both the U.S. House of Representatives and the U.S. Senate. He is recognized in Capitol Inside’s 2025 Texas Lobby Power Rankings, which ranks the top lobbyists in Texas.

In addition to his legislative background, he has a strong understanding of political strategy and grassroots mobilization from work on local, statewide, and congressional campaigns across the country. He has built an expansive network in all three branches of Texas government and a record of bringing legislative and industry leaders together on transportation and infrastructure, technology, natural resources, licensing and regulated industries, and utility-related issues.

Originally from Mississippi and an alumnus of the University of Mississippi (Ole Miss), Adam began his career as a Legislative Assistant in the Mississippi State Senate. In 2006 he joined then-Congressman Roger Wicker in the U.S. House and stayed on staff when Wicker became a U.S. Senator. He also served as Campaign Coordinator on Senator Wicker’s 2008 Senate campaign.

In 2008 Adam moved to Austin and joined the Office of Community Affairs for then-Governor Rick Perry, representing the Governor’s legislative agenda to state agencies and trade associations. He later served as Tarrant County Field Director on Governor Perry’s 2010 re-election campaign.

Adam served nine years in the Texas Senate, most recently as Chief of Staff to Senator Kelly Hancock. He earlier served Senator Hancock as Legislative Director and Senate Business & Commerce Committee Director, and began in the Senate as a Legislative Assistant to the late Senator Chris Harris.

He has helped shepherd legislation that established Texas as a leader in autonomous vehicle technology, transportation and infrastructure, insurance, alcohol-related issues, 5G technology, and related subjects.

Adam lives in Austin with his wife Kimberly and their two children, Phillip and Maggie.
